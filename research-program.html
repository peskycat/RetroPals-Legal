Research Program — Informed Consent

Applies to: RetroPals research participants Last updated: 20 July 2026 Contact: 4dlogan@gmail.com

This document explains what it means to take part in the RetroPals research program, so that you can decide, freely and with full information, whether to opt in. It supplements — and does not replace — the RetroPals Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

In short

Taking part is completely optional, has no effect on your ability to use the core RetroPals app, and you can withdraw at any time from Settings → Research Program without giving a reason and without penalty.

1. Who is conducting this research

This research is conducted by the developer of RetroPals as part of a Master of Science thesis in Usability Engineering at Hochschule Rhein-Waal (Rhein-Waal University of Applied Sciences), Germany, and may continue as part of ongoing future studies beyond the initial thesis.

Ethics approval

[Ethics approval reference to be added once granted by the relevant Hochschule Rhein-Waal ethics review process.] If you have concerns about the ethical conduct of this research, you may raise them with the researcher using the contact details in Section 13, or with Hochschule Rhein-Waal directly.

2. Purpose of the research

This research aims to understand how a structured wellbeing-reflection app — including AI companion chat, goal setting, and analytics — affects users' self-reported wellbeing, habits, and engagement over time. Findings will initially support a Master's thesis, and de-identified data may also be used in ongoing or future related studies on personal wellbeing tools, as described in Section 10.

3. Voluntary participation

Deciding whether to take part is entirely up to you:

  • You choose whether to opt in during onboarding (Step 1 — Research Program).
  • You can change your decision at any time from Settings → Research Program.
  • Declining, or later withdrawing, does not restrict your access to any feature of RetroPals — the app remains fully usable either way.
  • You will not receive payment or compensation for participating.

4. What participation involves

If you opt in, you are randomly assigned to a research group (for example, group A or B), which may determine minor variations in which settings or features are surfaced to you, so that the study can compare outcomes across groups. Beyond this, participation requires no extra tasks — you simply use RetroPals as you normally would: completing weekly reflections, chatting with AI companions, and tracking goals.

When you complete a weekly reflection while enrolled, a copy of that reflection's data may be automatically exported to a secure, access-restricted research record for analysis. This happens automatically in the background and does not change how you use the app.

5. What research data is collected

For enrolled participants only, the research record may include:

  • Your research group assignment and enrollment status;
  • Weekly reflection content: wellbeing, emotion, motivation and stress ratings, notes, and any attached weather/health data you chose to include;
  • Goal and challenge completion patterns;
  • Coarse usage information (such as how consistently reflections are completed over time).

AI chat conversation content is not included in the research export. Directly identifying information such as your name or email address is not included in the research dataset beyond an internal participant/group identifier needed to link your entries over time.

6. How your research data is handled

  • Research data is exported to a secure, access-controlled record used only for this research; the export destination can be updated by the researcher without requiring an app update, but access remains restricted to the researcher.
  • Data is stored on infrastructure located in the EU and handled in line with GDPR.
  • Only the researcher (and, where required, academic supervisors at Hochschule Rhein-Waal) have access to the identifiable research dataset.
  • Any results shared publicly (thesis, papers, presentations) will report data in aggregated and/or de-identified form — individual participants will not be identifiable in published outputs.

7. Risks and benefits

We do not anticipate significant risks from participation beyond those of using the RetroPals app itself. Reflecting on personal wellbeing topics can occasionally bring up difficult feelings; RetroPals' AI features are non-clinical and are not designed to manage this (see Section 11). There is no guaranteed direct personal benefit from participating; the anticipated benefit is a contribution to research on wellbeing reflection tools that may help improve RetroPals and inform future wellbeing app design.

8. Confidentiality

Your research data is kept confidential and is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing or unrelated purposes. It is used solely for the research purposes described in this document, and handled under the same security principles described in our Privacy Policy.

9. Your right to withdraw

You can withdraw from the research program at any time by turning off research participation in Settings → Research Program. If you withdraw, we will stop exporting new reflection data for you going forward. You may also request that previously exported research data associated with you be deleted by contacting 4dlogan@gmail.com; we will honour such requests except where data has already been irreversibly aggregated/anonymised into published results, at which point it can no longer be individually identified or removed.

10. Future studies

Beyond the initial Master's thesis, de-identified or aggregated data collected through this program may be retained and used for related ongoing or future academic studies on personal wellbeing tools conducted by the researcher. Where future research materially changes how identifiable data is used, we will update this document and, where required, seek renewed consent.

11. Not a clinical or emergency service

Important

Neither RetroPals nor this research program provides clinical, medical, or mental health services, and no member of the research team monitors participant entries for crisis intervention purposes. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency or crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline in your area — do not rely on RetroPals or the research team for this.

12. Consent acknowledgment

By opting in to the Research Program in the RetroPals app, you confirm that you:

  • are at least 16 years old;
  • have read and understood this Informed Consent document;
  • understand participation is voluntary and can be withdrawn at any time without penalty;
  • understand what data is collected and how it will be used, as described above;
  • consent to your reflection data being used for the research purposes described in this document.

Your in-app opt-in choice is recorded as your consent to this document. You do not need to sign anything outside the app.

13. Contact & questions

If you have any questions about the research program, your data, or this consent document, contact the researcher at 4dlogan@gmail.com.
