There is no research programme running at this time

No study is currently open and no research data is being collected from anyone using RetroPals.

RetroPals is developed alongside academic research into wellbeing and reflection, but the research program that was previously described here has been withdrawn. There is nothing to opt in to, and the app collects no data for research purposes.

If a study is offered in future it will require its own explicit consent — describing that specific study, what it collects, how long it runs, and how to withdraw. This page will be updated with a new consent document before any study opens.

If you have any questions, contact 4dlogan@gmail.com.
