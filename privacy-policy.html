4. Local-first storage & optional cloud sync

RetroPals is built local-first. By default, all of your data — reflections, goals, agents, profile, conversations — is stored securely on your own device (using an on-device database on mobile/desktop, or protected browser storage on web) and the app is fully usable offline. Nothing is sent to our servers for storage unless you explicitly turn on Back up & sync in Settings → Account.

Why this matters

Keeping data on-device by default minimises the amount of personal data we hold, gives you direct control over your information, and reduces the risk associated with any single data breach. You can use the core features of RetroPals without ever syncing data to the cloud.

If you opt in to sync, your data is mirrored to a secure, access-controlled cloud database (Firestore) so it can follow you across devices and be recovered if you lose your device. Some device-specific settings (theme, reminder preferences, local weather cache) always stay on-device only, even with sync enabled, because they are not meaningful to sync.

5. AI features & Zero Data Retention

RetroPals includes optional AI features: AI companion chat and AI-generated analysis of your weekly reflections. These are designed to offer general wellbeing support and encouragement — they are not clinical, medical, diagnostic, or therapeutic services, and they are not a substitute for professional care. See our Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.

We select flagship AI models chosen for strong safety guardrails, and we configure our AI integrations to route requests through providers operating under a Zero Data Retention (ZDR) policy wherever available.

What Zero Data Retention means

Under a ZDR arrangement, the content you send to an AI model (your prompts and the model's generated responses) is processed only in temporary memory to produce your response, and is not stored, logged, or used to train the model provider's future models. Your reflections and chat messages are used to generate your response and are then discarded by the model provider.

If you enable Bring Your Own AI (BYO AI) and connect your own OpenRouter API key, your AI requests are made using your own account and are subject to OpenRouter's and your chosen model provider's own data handling terms; we encourage you to review those directly, as we cannot guarantee the data practices of models or providers you select yourself outside our default configuration.

RetroPals itself does not use your reflections or chat content to train any AI model.

7. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for optional features such as cloud sync, automatic health data collection, location/weather, and research participation. You may withdraw consent at any time via in-app settings.
  • Performance of a contract — to provide the core app functionality you request, including account creation and paid subscriptions/credits.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the app secure, diagnose technical problems, and improve the product, balanced against your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain certain records (e.g. transaction records) to comply with tax or consumer-protection law.

9. International data transfers

Our primary cloud database is hosted in an EU region, so data you opt to sync stays within the EU/EEA. Some processors we use (including some AI model providers) may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, and — for AI processing in particular — Zero Data Retention arrangements that mean your content is not retained by the provider after your response is generated.

10. Data security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved, including:

  • Local-first storage that keeps most data off our servers entirely by default.
  • Encryption of BYO AI API keys (AES-256-GCM) using keys managed in a dedicated secrets manager, never exposed to any client device.
  • Access-controlled cloud database rules that restrict every synced user's data to that user only.
  • Payment processing delegated entirely to PCI-compliant providers (Apple, Google, Stripe, RevenueCat) — we never handle raw card details.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices proportionate to a personal wellbeing app.

11. Data retention

Data stored only on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Synced data is retained for as long as your account is active, or until you delete it via Settings → Manage Data, delete specific entries, or request account deletion by contacting us. Research data is retained as described in the Research Informed Consent document. We retain limited transaction records for as long as required by applicable tax/accounting law.

12. Your rights under GDPR

If the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") — you can do this yourself in-app via Settings → Manage Data, or by contacting us.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for anything based on consent (sync, health data, location, research participation), without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at 4dlogan@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.
